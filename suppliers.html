Supplier opportunity

A professional membership pathway for gemstone and jewellery suppliers.

The Guild is designed for suppliers who want to operate with greater credibility, organization and professional standards. Approved members receive a digital Guild membership card and may submit gemstones and jewellery for Guild review and potential opportunities. The Guild may introduce a member to a prospective buyer when a suitable opportunity arises, but membership does not guarantee a specific number of introductions, inquiries, transactions or sales.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide through membership applications, contact forms, newsletter signups or direct communications, including your name, business name, email address, phone number and information about your gemstone or jewellery business. When reasonably necessary to verify an application, we may also request government-issued identification, business registration information or supporting product documentation.

How we use information

We use this information to review membership applications, verify identity or business information, prevent fraud, administer memberships and renewals, communicate with members and applicants, provide Guild services, maintain records and send updates that you have requested.

Payments and third-party services

Payments, application forms and supporting records may be processed or stored by authorized third-party service providers involved in payment processing, membership administration, document handling or verification. We do not intend to store full payment-card details on this website.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with authorized service providers when needed to operate the Guild, process applications, verify information or comply with legal obligations. Identification documents will not be shared with potential buyers or referring members without permission, unless disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Data retention and protection

We retain personal information and identification documents only for as long as reasonably required for their stated purposes, membership administration, business records or applicable legal obligations. Access is limited to authorized individuals or service providers, and we use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. You may also unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at memberships@canadiangemtradeguild.ca.

Associate Supplier Membership Terms

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Membership Purpose

The Associate Supplier Membership program is operated by Canadian Gem Trade Guild. References to “the Guild,” “we,” “us,” or “our” mean Canadian Gem Trade Guild.

The Canadian Gem Trade Guild provides a structured membership program for gemstone and jewellery suppliers interested in exploring opportunities within the Canadian market.

The Guild verifies approved suppliers, receives submissions and may introduce a member to a prospective buyer when a suitable opportunity arises. The Guild is not a public marketplace or general broker.

2. Membership Eligibility

Associate Supplier Membership is available to individuals and businesses supplying gemstones and jewellery, subject to Guild requirements. Rough stones are not accepted.

Applicants must provide accurate and truthful information about their identity, business activities and products. Suppliers are responsible for the accuracy and authenticity of the information, representations, documentation and descriptions they provide.

Applicants are required to provide full name, business name where applicable, address, phone number, email address, government-issued identification and business registration information. The types of gemstones supplied are also required. Website or social media information is optional.

Other membership tiers, including Verified Supplier and Guild Trade Partner memberships, may be subject to additional eligibility requirements, fees and separate terms or guidelines.

3. Membership Fee

The current Associate Supplier Membership fee is CAD $149 per year. Membership pricing may be subject to change.

The applicable membership fee will be disclosed before payment. Existing members will be notified of any change to their renewal fee before the next scheduled renewal.

Payment is completed first. The applicant then submits the required application information, and the Guild may request identification or other supporting information by email before reviewing the application.

Payment does not guarantee membership approval and does not represent a gemstone purchase, deposit, investment or guarantee of future business.

4. Application Review

Applications are reviewed by the Guild after the required application information and verification materials have been received.

The Guild may request government-issued identification, business registration or other supporting information by email as part of identity and business verification.

The Guild aims to provide an initial response within 12 business days of receiving a completed application. Additional time may be required if further information or verification is necessary.

5. Approval and Membership Period

Membership becomes active once the application has been approved and the applicant receives written confirmation from the Guild. The digital membership card is then provided by email.

The annual billing period begins on the date the initial membership payment is processed. Membership benefits become available upon approval and remain active for the remainder of the current paid annual billing period.

If an application is declined, the subscription will be cancelled and the initial membership fee will be refunded in full.

6. Refund Policy

If a membership application is declined, the membership fee will be refunded in full.

After membership has been approved and activated, the annual membership fee is generally non-refundable, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

A lack of purchasing opportunities, product submissions, offers or completed sales does not automatically entitle a member to a refund.

7. Submissions and Opportunities

Membership does not guarantee:

  • A specific number of introductions or inquiries.
  • A buyer for any submission.
  • The purchase of any gemstone or jewellery.
  • A minimum sales volume, purchase price or profit.
  • A particular transaction value or frequency.

The Guild may introduce a member to a prospective buyer when a suitable opportunity arises. If a submission does not attract interest, the Guild may work with the supplier to identify improvements to its market presentation, including pricing, documentation, photography, descriptions or other relevant factors. Any opportunity depends on buyer requirements, product suitability, pricing, documentation, market conditions and other relevant considerations.

8. Product Information and Disclosure

Members must provide accurate and complete information about products submitted for consideration. This includes, where applicable:

  • Gemstone type, weight and dimensions.
  • Known treatments or enhancements.
  • Whether a gemstone is natural, laboratory-grown, synthetic or imitation.
  • Country of origin, when known.
  • Laboratory reports or certifications.
  • Product condition, availability and asking price.

The Guild may decline to consider products where information is incomplete, misleading, unverifiable or inconsistent with applicable requirements.

When the Guild itself has a specific gemstone purchasing need, appropriate certification or supporting documentation is required for gemstones the Guild purchases or considers for that specific need.

9. Purchasing Arrangements

The Guild may introduce suppliers to prospective buyers. After an introduction, the supplier and buyer communicate and arrange any private transaction directly with one another.

Any purchase or sale must be agreed upon separately by the supplier and buyer, including price, inspection, payment, shipping, insurance, customs, delivery, warranties, returns, contracts, taxes and other transaction terms.

The Guild does not charge suppliers a commission on private transactions resulting from a Guild introduction. The supplier and buyer remain responsible for their own transaction terms and applicable obligations.

Members must not ship gemstones or jewellery unless they have received clear written instructions regarding the proposed shipment.

10. Identification and Privacy

Applicants may be asked to provide government-issued identification or business registration information for identity verification, membership administration and fraud prevention.

Personal information and identification documents will be accessed only by authorized individuals or service providers involved in membership administration or verification.

Identification documents will not be shared with potential buyers or referring members without permission, unless disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes or applicable legal obligations.

Membership and application communication is handled by email. Privacy-related questions may be directed to memberships@canadiangemtradeguild.ca.

11. Confidentiality

Members must respect the confidentiality of information shared during the membership and purchasing process.

This includes non-public information relating to buyers, suppliers, pricing, product evaluations and business arrangements.

The Guild is not required to disclose the identities or contact information of potential buyers or other suppliers.

12. Use of Guild Name and Materials

Members may use the Guild's name, logo, membership badges, digital IDs, membership cards, listings or other Guild materials only when authorized by the Guild and in accordance with any applicable conditions or guidelines.

Members must not misrepresent their membership category, claim a verification or partnership status they have not received, or suggest that the Guild has endorsed a product, guaranteed a purchase or approved a business relationship without authorization.

Use of the Guild's name or materials does not transfer ownership of the Guild's name, logo, website content or other intellectual property. Members must stop using Guild materials when their membership is suspended or terminated, or when the Guild withdraws its authorization.

13. Membership Renewal

Associate Supplier Membership is an automatically renewing annual subscription.

By purchasing a membership, the member authorizes Canadian Gem Trade Guild to charge the payment method provided for the applicable annual membership fee upon each renewal. The current annual membership fee is CAD $149.

Membership will automatically renew every 12 months on the anniversary of the original payment date unless the member cancels before the next scheduled renewal date.

Members may cancel their subscription at any time by contacting memberships@canadiangemtradeguild.ca. Cancellation prevents future renewal charges, but the membership remains active until the end of the current paid membership period.

Membership fees that have already been charged are non-refundable, except where required by applicable law.

Any changes to the annual membership fee will be communicated before the next renewal charge, and members may cancel before the new charge takes effect.

If an application is declined, the subscription will be cancelled and the initial membership fee will be refunded in full.

14. Suspension or Termination

The Canadian Gem Trade Guild reserves the right to suspend or terminate a membership at any time if a member:

  • Fails to pay an applicable membership or renewal fee.
  • Provides false or misleading information.
  • Conceals gemstone treatments or other material product details.
  • Submits fraudulent identification, certifications or documentation.
  • Engages in unlawful, abusive or inappropriate conduct.
  • Misuses the Guild’s name, reputation or confidential information.
  • Fails to follow applicable membership requirements or policies.
  • Engages in any activity that may compromise the integrity, safety or reputation of the Guild, its members or its buyers.

Where appropriate, a member may be given an opportunity to respond before a final decision is made. However, membership may be suspended or terminated without prior notice where appropriate.

Members whose memberships are suspended or terminated are not entitled to a refund of their membership fees, except where required by applicable law.

Upon termination, all membership privileges and access to Guild-related opportunities end immediately.

15. Acceptance of Terms

By accepting these terms at checkout, proceeding with payment and submitting a membership application, the applicant confirms that they have read, understood and accepted these Associate Supplier Membership Terms.
